## Known limitations

- Not notarized; the first open needs "Open Anyway" (documented in the README).
- arm64 only.
- Memory numbers are approximate (public APIs, shared pages counted in every process).
- The cleanup record is saved at the end of a cleanup; a crash in the middle loses the Undo for items already moved.
- Not verified here (the terminal has no Screen Recording or click permission): closing the menu bar panel with a real click stops the sampling (`SPARE_DEBUG_SAMPLES`), and Cmd+Q during a cleanup. Both go to the real Mac run.
- Dark theme only; no Homebrew tap.

## Follow-ups for v0.2

From the review ledger (all minor):

- Yarn through corepack (`yarn.js`) is not matched to `yarn` in the in-use check; `dev.homebrew` process name `brew` likely never matches (`brew.sh`, `brew.rb`), consider checking command lines.
- The in-use watchdog may terminate an already exited `ps` (fails closed); `processes()` runs synchronously inside the async scan (up to about 15 s worst case).
- Command runner: a grandchild holding the pipe leaks a reader thread, and a timeout signals the child, not the process group.
- Cleaner: a partial failure while emptying the Trash does not record what was already deleted; `.changed` items stay `.done`, so `purge` repeats the failure each time.
- Undo does not run `originalPath` through `SafetyGuard` (only exploitable with a forged history); a save failure after undo hides that files were restored.
- Incremental per-item history during a cleanup (crash or kill); history and report I/O run on the main actor at the end of a cleanup.
- Process terminator: reused-pid check skipped when the listed path is not absolute, uid read from the listing only; same-executable pid reuse between the listing and the confirmation.
- Largest files lists hard-linked files once per link.
- UI: blank flicker of the current path while scanning; a reopened panel may wait up to 5 s for memory; `ps` every 15 s while the window is open in any section; a forced refresh after a quit may be skipped while one is in flight; `isCleaning` is not published.
- Test support: `RecordingCommandRunner` has unchecked `Sendable` vars.
- Product: universal (x86_64) build, notarization, light theme, Homebrew tap.
